# Phased build prompts

## Phase 1 — Paid concierge proof

“Add a single-kit reservation flow backed by Stripe test mode and a simple Postgres inventory window. Require a $10 refundable reservation, pickup date and explicit demo/legal copy. Use idempotency keys, transactional holds and automated expiry. Test simultaneous attempts for the same kit.”

## Phase 2 — Turn operations

“Add numbered physical kits, before/after component checklists, inspection photos, turnaround status and handling-time capture. Make missing components and damage named events with audit history. Build an operator queue for pickup, due, overdue, inspection and repair.”

## Phase 3 — Customer lifecycle

“Add authenticated rental history, due-date reminders, return instructions and hold release. Instrument quote-to-paid, utilization, late rate, damage rate, handling minutes and contribution margin. Provide a runbook and alerts for payment compensation and stuck holds.”

## Phase 4 — Evidence-gated expansion

“Only after 20 turns meet the product-plan thresholds, add one adjacent audio kit. Compare outcome-led versus gear-led merchandising. Do not add marketplace supply, national shipping or AI guidance until the second category repeats acceptable payback and handling.”
